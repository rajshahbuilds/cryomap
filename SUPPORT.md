# Support

CryoMap is a public beta, maintained on a **best-effort basis with no guaranteed
response time**. It is provided as-is under the [Apache-2.0](./LICENSE) license,
without warranty.

## Where to go

- **Questions & ideas** — open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/rajshahbuilds/cryomap/issues)
  using the feature-request or a blank template, or start a discussion if
  Discussions are enabled.
- **Bugs** — open an issue with the bug template; include steps to reproduce,
  what you expected, and what happened.
- **Security problems** — do **not** file a public issue; follow
  [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
- **Running your own instance** — see [`docs/SELF_HOSTING.md`](./docs/SELF_HOSTING.md).
  Help with self-hosted deployments is best-effort and community-driven.

## What to expect

- Issues are triaged when time allows; there is no SLA.
- The official hosted beta (https://cryomap.vercel.app) may change or have
  downtime without notice during beta.
- Back up anything important — exports are available per diagram.
