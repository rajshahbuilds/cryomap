# CryoMap Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026

This policy applies to the free CryoMap beta hosted at
[cryomap.vercel.app](https://cryomap.vercel.app) and operated by Raj Shah
("CryoMap", "we", or "us"). It does not apply to independently operated,
self-hosted deployments of the open-source software.

## Information we collect

- **Account information.** When you sign in with Google, we receive and store
  your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and avatar URL.
  We do not receive or store your Google password.
- **Workspace and diagram information.** We store the workspaces you create or
  join, membership roles, invitations, diagrams, components, connections,
  annotations, versions, notes, authorship information, and timestamps needed
  to provide the service.
- **Session and preference information.** We use cookies required to keep you
  signed in and remember your active workspace. CryoMap does not currently use
  advertising cookies or third-party analytics.
- **Operational information.** Our hosting and database providers may process
  standard technical information such as IP addresses, request metadata, and
  error logs to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

## How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; provide workspace access; store,
version, display, and export diagrams; maintain security; troubleshoot the
service; and respond to support or data requests.

## Workspace access

Diagram information is available only to members of its workspace, according
to their role:

- **Admin:** full access, including member, invitation, and workspace
  management.
- **Editor:** can view and edit diagrams.
- **Viewer:** can view diagrams.

Access is enforced using database row-level security. Workspace administrators
and members are responsible for managing invitations and access appropriately.

## Service providers

The hosted service relies on:

- **Supabase** for authentication and database services;
- **Vercel** for application hosting and delivery; and
- **Google** for sign-in and identity information.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.
We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably
necessary to protect the security and integrity of CryoMap or its users.

## Retention and deletion

We keep account, workspace, and diagram information while it is needed to
provide the service. A workspace administrator can permanently delete a
workspace and its diagrams from Workspace Settings. Members can leave a
workspace to remove their access.

To request deletion of your CryoMap account information, email
**rajshahbuilds@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your account.
Deleting an account does not necessarily delete workspace information that
other members continue to use. Provider logs and backup copies may remain for a
limited period after deletion before they are rotated or deleted.

## Your choices

You can export diagrams you are permitted to view as PNG, PDF, or JSON. You may
also request access to or correction of your account information, or ask a
question about how it is handled, by contacting us.

## Security

We use Google OAuth, HTTPS, and workspace-scoped row-level security. No online
service can guarantee absolute security. Please follow the private reporting
instructions in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) if you discover a vulnerability.

## Changes

We may update this policy as CryoMap changes. The effective date above will be
updated when a revised policy takes effect. If a change materially affects how
we handle personal information, we will provide appropriate notice.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact **rajshahbuilds@gmail.com**.
