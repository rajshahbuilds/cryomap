# CryoMap Trademark & Branding Policy

> **Draft policy — not legal advice.** This is a starting-point branding policy
> for the CryoMap project; have it reviewed by qualified counsel before relying
> on it, and see the [conflict-search note](#name-conflict-search) below.

CryoMap's **source code** is free and open under the
[Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE). This trademark policy is **separate from** that
license: the Apache-2.0 grant covers copyright and patents, **not** trademark
rights (see Apache-2.0 §6). Nothing here restricts your rights to the code under
Apache-2.0 — it only governs use of the CryoMap **name and branding** so users
can tell the official project and service apart from forks and third-party
deployments.

## The marks

"**CryoMap**", the CryoMap name and logo, and the identity of the official hosted
service at **https://cryomap.vercel.app** are marks of the CryoMap project
(Raj Shah). They are not licensed under Apache-2.0.

## What you can always do

- **Use, modify, fork, and self-host the code** under Apache-2.0.
- **Refer to CryoMap truthfully** (nominative use): e.g. "based on CryoMap", "a
  fork of CryoMap", "compatible with CryoMap", "powered by CryoMap". Keep such
  references accurate and non-prominent relative to your own branding.
- **Keep the name in unmodified copies** and in the LICENSE/NOTICE files (you
  must, under Apache-2.0).
- **State that your build derives from CryoMap** in docs and release notes.

## What you may not do

- **Imply official status, endorsement, affiliation, or partnership** with the
  CryoMap project or its maintainers when none exists.
- **Present a fork or a third-party deployment as the official CryoMap** service,
  or use the CryoMap name/logo as the primary branding of a public service.
- **Use the CryoMap name or branding for a substantially modified public
  service.** If you run a modified version as a public product, please give it a
  **different product name** and your own branding; a truthful "based on CryoMap"
  line is fine.
- **Register or claim** "CryoMap" (or confusingly similar names/logos/domains) as
  your own mark.

## Official vs. third-party

The only official, maintained CryoMap is the project in this repository and the
hosted beta at https://cryomap.vercel.app. Any other deployment is operated by a
third party, is not endorsed by the project, and is the operator's
responsibility.

## Name conflict search

A preliminary, informal web search (2026-10) found **no existing software product
or trademark using the exact name "CryoMap."** Adjacent cryo-domain names exist
(e.g. CryoFlow, CryoTrack's CRYO-\* marks, KaryoMap) but differ in name and field.
This is **not** a legal clearance search: before any trademark registration or
broad public launch, run a formal availability search (e.g. USPTO and relevant
national registries) and consult counsel.

## Future registration

Trademark registration for "CryoMap" is **not** being pursued now. It remains an
option the project may take up if adoption grows, which would strengthen the
protections described here.
